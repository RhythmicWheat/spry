Spry privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Spry is a health and fitness tracker for workouts, food, water, energy, sleep and steps. It is built to keep your information on your phone. This policy explains what Spry stores, what leaves your phone, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • Your data stays on your phone. Spry has no accounts and no servers that hold your data. Everything you log is stored in a database on your device.
  • We don't collect, sell or share your personal information. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking code in Spry.
  • Only food searches go online. When you search for a branded food or scan a barcode, that search is sent to Open Food Facts to look up the product.
  • You're in control. You can back up your data to a file, restore it, or delete all of it at any time from the You tab.

What Spry stores on your phone

Everything you enter or that Spry reads with your permission is stored only on your device:

  • Profile and settings: year of birth, sex, height, weight, goal, activity level, nutrition targets, units, reminder and rest timer settings.
  • Workouts: routines, exercises, sets, weights, reps, times and personal records.
  • Food and drink: foods you log, saved meals, your own foods and water.
  • Wellbeing: your daily energy rating.
  • Workouts from other apps: if you use Connected devices, workouts recorded by a watch or another app that Spry reads from Apple Health (time, type, distance, heart rate and calories).

This information never leaves your phone unless you choose to export it in a backup file.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, Spry asks for permission to read:

  • steps
  • sleep
  • heart rate and resting heart rate
  • weight
  • active energy
  • workouts

You choose which of these to allow, and you can change this at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps → Spry, or in iPhone Settings.

Spry uses Apple Health data only to show it to you inside the app and to avoid tracking the same thing twice (for example, linking a workout from your watch with one you logged in Spry). Data from Apple Health:

  • stays on your phone
  • is never sent to us or anyone else
  • is never used for advertising or marketing
  • is never sold

Spry does not currently write any data to Apple Health. If a future version adds this, it will ask for your permission first and this policy will be updated.

Information that leaves your phone

Open Food Facts (food search and barcodes)

When you search for a branded food or scan a barcode, Spry sends the search words or the barcode number to Open Food Facts, a free, open food database. Spry sends nothing else about you: no name, account, health data or location. Like any website, Open Food Facts receives your device's IP address when it answers the request. Open Food Facts handles that under its own privacy policy.

Searching the general foods built into Spry happens on your phone and sends nothing online.

Backup files you create

If you use Back up to a file, Spry creates a file containing your data and opens the share sheet. Where that file goes is your choice (for example, Files, iCloud Drive or an email to yourself). Once you save it somewhere, that service's own privacy policy applies to the file.

Camera

Spry uses the camera only to scan food barcodes. Scanning happens on your phone. Spry does not take, save or send photos.

Notifications

Reminders and rest timer alerts are scheduled on your phone. They are all off until you turn them on, and Spry does not use push notifications from a server.

Deleting your data

  • Delete all my data on the You tab permanently removes everything you've logged and your settings from Spry.
  • Deleting the Spry app from your phone also deletes all of its data.
  • Backup files you've saved elsewhere aren't affected. Delete those yourself if you no longer want them.

Because we never receive your data, there's nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Spry isn't designed for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If Spry starts handling information differently, we'll update this policy and change the date at the top before the new version is released. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
